A SMALL GUIDE

From word to memory

  1. Choose meanings. Words come from a-concept-lab. Domain memberships coexist throughout one continuous volume.
  2. Teach the network. Word cues and teaching signals strengthen connections to a three-cell assembly—our experimental engram.
  3. Send Sense Input. One encoded word cue reaches every enrolled domain. Output collects the measured responses. A memory is recalled when all three cells activate. Multiple matches stay ambiguous.

What the visualization shows
Neuron’s original procedural soma, axon and dendrite shapes are drawn at actual neuron positions. These shapes are illustrative; synaptic lines show stored connections. After recall, the animation replays measured spikes at a slower pace.

Fact learning supports explicit color, location and category statements with forward/reverse questions. Shared concept cells and learned relationship links drive recall. This is a controlled grammar, not open-ended language understanding. Text learning stores supervised question/response examples and reads output tokens from measured spikes. It preserves earlier lessons and word order, but does not infer arbitrary facts or generalize to paraphrases. Vocabulary mode keeps its original locked memories and reference definitions.
